ELEVATEX: BUSINESS WEBSITE READINESS CHECKLIST
For service businesses serving Pakistan
Version: 23 October 2026

Companion guide:
https://elevatexlab.org/insights/what-makes-a-website-convert-in-2026/

Use this editable worksheet before commissioning or reviewing a website.
Mark each item PASS, NEEDS WORK, NOT CHECKED or NOT APPLICABLE. A tick is
not a score or a certification. Keep a short evidence record for each
important decision. This worksheet does not predict rankings or sales.

A. THE OFFER
[ ] 01. A new visitor can explain the actual service in their own words.
[ ] 02. The page identifies the intended customer and the limits of scope.
[ ] 03. The primary button describes what its destination actually does.
[ ] 04. Each important claim has evidence the visitor can inspect.
[ ] 05. Concept projects and demonstrations are clearly labelled.
[ ] 06. Every testimonial, client name or result has permission and a source.

B. SERVING CUSTOMERS IN PAKISTAN
[ ] 07. Service coverage matches where the business genuinely operates.
[ ] 08. No office, local address, certification or affiliation is invented.
[ ] 09. Language choices match what the team can actually support.
[ ] 10. If Urdu is included, a fluent reader checks meaning and right-to-left layout.
[ ] 11. Any published prices state the actual currency and approved inclusions.
[ ] 12. Contact details, business hours and availability are accurate.
[ ] 13. Any WhatsApp link opens the intended business conversation on a real phone.
[ ] 14. Someone owns incoming enquiries, including those received out of hours.

C. MOBILE AND ACCESSIBILITY
[ ] 15. The complete task works on a real phone without horizontal scrolling.
[ ] 16. Text remains readable when enlarged; menus and notices do not cover actions.
[ ] 17. Keyboard users can reach, identify and activate every important control.
[ ] 18. Form fields have visible labels and errors explain how to recover.
[ ] 19. Essential text, contact routes and navigation work without JavaScript.
[ ] 20. Reduced motion does not remove content or block the visitor's task.
[ ] 21. Images reserve space and meaningful images have useful text alternatives.

D. ENQUIRIES AND FOLLOW-THROUGH
[ ] 22. An actual test submission reaches the person responsible for responding.
[ ] 23. The success state matches what the system has really accepted.
[ ] 24. A failed delivery has a useful recovery route or accurate explanation.
[ ] 25. Server-side validation and practical abuse controls protect the form.
[ ] 26. Only information needed for the next decision is requested.
[ ] 27. Privacy information explains the actual handling of the enquiry.
[ ] 28. Analytics do not receive names, email addresses or message contents.

E. SEARCH DISCOVERY AND MAINTENANCE
[ ] 29. Approved public pages have one clear H1 and useful titles/descriptions.
[ ] 30. Canonicals use the chosen HTTPS hostname and point to the right page.
[ ] 31. Approved public pages are indexable; private/draft pages stay protected.
[ ] 32. HTTP and alternate-host URLs redirect to the chosen HTTPS hostname.
[ ] 33. Important pages have relevant internal links and work when opened directly.
[ ] 34. Sitemap entries are canonical, successful, approved public pages.
[ ] 35. Important old URLs have reviewed equivalent destinations when moved.
[ ] 36. A maintenance owner can update content, check enquiries and recover a release.

PRIORITY
First: a broken enquiry, inaccessible essential task or misleading claim.
Next: discovery, content clarity and evidence gaps.
Then: visual refinements that support the visitor's decision.
